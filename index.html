About ELECT Project

An Erasmus+ Capacity Building in Higher Education (CBHE) initiative

The ELECT Project aims to improve access and quality of higher education in energy-efficient lighting at Zambian universities. By modernizing curricula, enhancing teaching infrastructure, and promoting collaboration between academia, industry, and the public sector, ELECT supports Zambia's green transition towards a low-carbon, sustainable economy.

Start: 1 Jan 2026
Duration: 3 Years
Coordinator: Aalto University, FI

Our Core Objectives

Aligning with ELECT's three strategic goals

Modernize Courses & Hands-On Learning

Develop and update bachelor's/diploma curricula with virtual labs, DIALux simulations, and digital resources for smart lighting education.

Access Resources

Enhance Teaching Infrastructure

Improve pedagogical expertise through faculty training, digital tools, and student-centered learning environments with modern lab equipment.
